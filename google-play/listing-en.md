## Full description (max 4000)
Azqm is a food diary that runs entirely on your phone — no sign-up, no ads, and your data never leaves the device. The app speaks 8 languages: English, Bulgarian, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Russian and Turkish.

🍽️ EASY FOOD LOGGING
• 1000+ foods with detailed nutrition data (USDA), including 120+ Bulgarian dishes
• Food names in your language; search works with or without accents
• One tap on "+" adds a portion; natural portions like "1 egg" or "1 slice"
• Favorites, recent foods, your own foods, copy the previous day
• Barcode scanning (Open Food Facts) and manual entry

🥑 14 DIETS
Keto, Atanas Uzunov's diet, low carb, low fat, balanced, Mediterranean, the Zone (40/30/30), DASH, high protein, paleo, vegetarian, vegan, carnivore and therapeutic fasting. Each diet has its own calorie split and food rules, and a colored dot next to every food shows whether it fits.

📊 ANALYSIS
• Average calories over 7, 30 or 90 days
• Protein, fat and carbs against your diet's targets
• Which vitamins and minerals you lack — and suitable foods to fix it
• What you overdo (salt, sugar, saturated fat…) and which foods it comes from
• Projected weight change at your current intake

⏱️ INTERMITTENT FASTING
Timer from 12:12 up to 72 hours, fasting stages, history, water and electrolytes.

⚖️ WEIGHT & PROGRESS
Weight chart, BMI and recalculation of your daily targets.

🔒 YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
Everything is stored on the phone only. Export your diary to Excel (.xlsx) or CSV and make backups you can restore later.

🎨 COMFORT
Dark and light theme, three text sizes, an easy-to-read font. Works on phones, tablets and foldables.

Azqm is not a medical device and does not replace medical advice. Talk to a doctor before extended fasting or a major change in your diet, especially with diabetes, pregnancy, breastfeeding or a chronic condition.
