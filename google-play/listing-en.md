## Full description (max 4000)
Azqm is a food diary in Bulgarian that runs entirely on your phone — no sign-up, no ads, and your data never leaves the device.

🍽️ EASY FOOD LOGGING
• 1000+ foods with detailed nutrition data (USDA), including 120+ Bulgarian dishes
• Search in Cyrillic or Latin letters
• One-tap "+" adds a portion; natural portions like "1 egg" or "1 slice"
• Favorites, recent foods, your own foods, copy yesterday
• Barcode scanning (Open Food Facts) and manual entry

🥑 14 DIETS
Keto, Atanas Uzunov's diet, low-carb, low-fat, balanced, Mediterranean, the Zone (40/30/30), DASH, high-protein, paleo, vegetarian, vegan, carnivore and therapeutic fasting — each with its own calorie split and food rules.

📊 ANALYSIS
• Average calories over 7, 30 or 90 days
• Protein, fat and carbs against your diet's targets
• Which vitamins and minerals you lack — and suitable foods to fix it
• What you overdo (salt, sugar, saturated fat…) and which foods cause it
• Projected weight change at your current intake

⏱️ INTERMITTENT FASTING
Timer from 12:12 up to 72 hours, fasting stages, history, water and electrolytes.

⚖️ WEIGHT & PROGRESS
Weight chart, BMI and goal recalculation.

🔒 YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
Everything is stored on the phone. Export to Excel (.xlsx) or CSV and make backups you can restore.

Azqm is not a medical device and does not replace medical advice. Talk to a doctor before extended fasting or major diet changes.
